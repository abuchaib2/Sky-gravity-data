We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the game. AdMob may collect and use data (such as advertising IDs and device information) to serve personalized ads. For more information on how Google uses this data, please visit the Google Privacy & Terms site.

4. Children's Privacy

Our game is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes.

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
